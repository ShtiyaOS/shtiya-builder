## 1. Architectural Overview & The Integration Strategy
The Architect and Engineer group provides the mathematical, spatial, and regulatory framework required to safely alter the physical environment[cite: 1]. Rather than forcing design professionals to abandon industry-standard tools, Shtiya Builder acts as a secure Common Data Environment (CDE) and synchronization hub.

*   **The API Integration Engine:** The platform utilizes integrations (e.g., Autodesk APIs, ESRI ArcGIS) to allow professionals to work in their native environments (AutoCAD, Revit). They simply "vault" their finalized files into the Shtiya ecosystem.
*   **Automated Data Parsing:** Once a 3D Building Information Model (BIM) is uploaded, Shtiya’s backend parses the metadata to automatically generate a deterministic Bill of Quantities (BoQ)[cite: 1]. This immediately connects the architect's design to the Developer's capital stack and the Supplier's material inventory.
*   **Monetization Engine:** Affiliate commissions for software license referrals (e.g., user buys an AutoCAD seat through the portal), alongside data-hosting fees for vaulting massive, gigabyte-heavy geotechnical and BIM files.

## 3. Sub-Role Portals & Feature Matrices

### A. Registered Architect (`arch_ra`)
Focuses on efficient intellectual property deployment, spatial design, and generating the baseline mathematical requirements for materials[cite: 1].
*   **BIM-to-Map Overlay:** The Interactive Map allows the architect to overlay their 3D BIM files directly onto the geographic topography[cite: 1]. Visual layers highlight strict municipal setbacks, D-Architectural Design Zone overlays, and maximum height restrictions[cite: 1].
*   **Automated BoQ Generation:** The system completely eliminates manual quantity take-offs[cite: 1]. The vault parses the uploaded CAD files and instantly outputs the exact lumber, concrete, and steel requirements to the group's shared ledger.
*   **Version Control:** Vaulting CAD files directly into the property's secure, RBAC-protected digital data room eliminates the severe risk of version control chaos across endless design iterations[cite: 1].

### B. Structural Engineer (`arch_engineer`)
The ultimate arbiter of physical safety, focusing strictly on physics, soil stability, and structural integrity[cite: 1].
*   **Geological Map View:** The map shifts to a deeply hydrological and geological perspective[cite: 1]. It renders specific fault line offsets, Alquist-Priolo Special Studies Zones, liquefaction indices, and precise topographic constraints (e.g., 1:1 to 5:1 horizontal to vertical slopes)[cite: 1].
*   **Oracle Sign-Off Protocol:** Centralizes massive soil reports and structural load calculations into a single immutable repository[cite: 1]. The engineer uses this portal to issue the cryptographic sign-offs required to validate foundation and framing integrity[cite: 1].

### C. Zoning Consultant (`arch_zoning`)
Navigates the highly political regulatory landscape, seeking lucrative variances, density bonuses, and block consolidations[cite: 1].
*   **Regulatory Matrix Map:** The layers display exact Floor Area Ratios, conditional use permit zones, and historical paths of city council voting behavior regarding variances[cite: 1]. Clicking a parcel immediately highlights the exact density arbitrage opportunity for the Master Assembler[cite: 1].
*   **Policy Codification Tool:** Removes the friction of manual policy analysis by codifying complex municipal zoning texts (like SB 9 Two Unit Projects) into highly searchable, spatial databases[cite: 1].
*   **Intent Aggregator:** Utilizes cryptographic intent-to-sell agreements generated by the Owner-Investor group to present a legally binding, unified front to the municipal planning commission[cite: 1].
