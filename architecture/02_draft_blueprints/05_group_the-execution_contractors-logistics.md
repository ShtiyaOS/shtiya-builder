# OFFICIAL BLUEPRINT: Group 5 - Contractor, Sub-Trade & Logistics
**Shtiya Builder Enterprise Architecture & Workspace Design**

## 1. Architectural Overview, Compliance & The Supply Chain
The Contractor, Sub-Trade, and Logistics group forms the physical execution layer of the platform[cite: 1]. This group turns digital blueprints into physical reality. To operate at scale and eliminate construction litigation and site congestion, the system automates trade licensing verification, Certificate of Insurance (COI) compliance, and Just-In-Time (JIT) material logistics.

*   **Automated Trade & License Verification:** Specialty trades require rigorous verification before accepting work. The platform integrates with municipal licensing databases to verify active status (e.g., Master Electrician, Master Plumber, Hoisting/Rigging).
*   **The COI & Bonding Vault:** Uninsured contractors represent catastrophic liability. The system requires contractors to vault digital Certificates of Insurance (COI). If a policy lapses, the smart contract system immediately locks the contractor's dispatch status and freezes draw releases until a renewed COI is verified.
*   **The Logistics Expansion:** Large urban projects lack "laydown yards" (on-site storage). The platform integrates Third-Party Logistics (3PL) warehousing and last-mile trucking directly into the Bill of Quantities (BoQ) workflow, allowing developers to lock in bulk material pricing and store it off-site for scheduled delivery.

## 3. Sub-Role Portals & Feature Matrices

### A. General Contractor (`contractor_gc`)
Oversees entire project scopes, manages sub-trades, and serves as the primary point of physical accountability[cite: 1].
*   **Logistical Job Map:** Monitors active site locations across the region[cite: 1]. Visual layers highlight material delivery zones, traffic restrictions, and local supply houses[cite: 1]. Clicking a site marker opens the project dashboard with the BoQ, active permits, and environmental requirements[cite: 1].
*   **Cash Flow Accelerator:** Submits progress updates backed by geotagged site photos to trigger automated AI computer-vision verifications and release milestone draws without 60-day delays[cite: 1].

### B. Specialty Sub-Contractor (`contractor_sub`)
Covers trade-specific workers—plumbing, electrical, HVAC, masonry, roofing, and structural steel.
*   **Task-Oriented Map View:** Hyper-local view rendering isolated work orders and maintenance tickets pinned to properties[cite: 1]. Clicking a marker opens trade-specific CAD schematics, safety protocols, and approved job budgets[cite: 1].
*   **Direct Payment Protection:** Completely eliminates GC payment withholding risk. Work packages are pre-funded in project escrows, guaranteeing direct payment upon verified completion[cite: 1].

### C. Material Supplier (`contractor_supplier`)
Connects raw materials, tools, and heavy equipment directly to active construction workflows[cite: 1].
*   **Demand Forecasting Map:** Displays material demand heatmaps based on real-time BoQs generated by architects across the platform network[cite: 1].
*   **In-App B2B Store:** Lists real-time inventory for instant developer and GC procurement[cite: 1].
*   **Instant AR Settlement:** Eliminates B2B credit risk and invoice chasing[cite: 1]. Orders execute against verified project escrow funds, settling value instantly[cite: 1].

### D. Logistics & Warehousing (`contractor_logistics`) [The New Addition]
Manages off-site storage, laydown yards, and Just-In-Time (JIT) last-mile delivery to congested urban job sites.
*   **Warehouse Capacity Map:** Renders available cubic footage in local 3PL warehouses. Developers can rent temporary staging space directly through the map.
*   **Fleet Routing & Crane Sync:** Integrates with the General Contractor's master schedule to coordinate exact ingress/egress routes. Ensures flatbed trucks only arrive when the tower crane or hoisting crew is actively ready to receive the load, preventing municipal traffic fines.

## 4. Smart Contract & Financial Triggers
*   **Proof-of-Delivery (PoD) Escrow Sweeps:** When the `contractor_logistics` truck arrives at the job site, the GC scans a digital QR bill of lading. This cryptographic Proof-of-Delivery instantly triggers the smart contract to release the final material payment to the supplier and the delivery fee to the logistics provider.
*   **AI Progress Verification:** Upon milestone submission, AI models analyze site photos against uploaded CAD models[cite: 1]. Upon verification, the smart contract releases $SHQL draw funds from escrow[cite: 1].
*   **Synchronous Split Payments:** When a GC receives an escrow draw, embedded smart contract logic automatically splits and routes pre-agreed amounts directly to the sub-contractors' wallets[cite: 1].
*   **Automated COI Freeze:** If a contractor's or logistics provider's insurance expires mid-project, the system automatically halts their platform dispatch status and freezes pending escrow releases until updated proof of insurance is verified.
